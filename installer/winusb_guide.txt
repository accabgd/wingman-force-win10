ONE MORE STEP: tell Windows to use the "WinUSB" driver for your joystick
=======================================================================

You only have to do this once. It takes about one minute.

1. Plug in the WingMan Force (and its power adapter).

2. Device Manager opens when you finish this setup.
   (Or press the Windows key + R, type   devmgmt.msc   and press Enter.)

3. Look for "Other devices" and click the small arrow next to it.
   You will see "Wingman Force" with a yellow warning triangle.

4. Right-click "Wingman Force" and choose "Update driver".

5. Click "Browse my computer for drivers".

6. Click "Let me pick from a list of available drivers on my computer".

7. Scroll down, click "Universal Serial Bus devices", then click "Next".

8. On the left (Manufacturer) click "WinUsb Device".
   On the right (Model) click "WinUsb Device". Click "Next".

9. If Windows warns that the driver may not be compatible, click "Yes".

Done! "Wingman Force" now appears under "Universal Serial Bus devices".

Now double-click the "WingMan Force" icon on your desktop and keep its window
open while you play. In Microsoft Flight Simulator, choose the joystick called
"WingMan Force J-UA9" and bind:
   Ailerons (Axis)  ->  Joystick X axis
   Elevator (Axis)  ->  Joystick Y axis
   Throttle         ->  Joystick Z axis
Do NOT start XPForce at the same time.

To undo: in Device Manager right-click the stick and choose "Uninstall device".
